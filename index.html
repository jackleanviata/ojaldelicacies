Our Story

Rooted in the Community

Ojal Groceries was born from a simple belief: every family in Embul Bul deserves access to fresh, affordable food without travelling far. We started as a small stall and have grown into a trusted neighbourhood institution — your go-to for boiled eggs at dawn, fresh fish in the evening, and everything in between.

We source directly from local suppliers to keep quality high and prices fair. When you shop here, you're also supporting Ngong's local food network.

🌿

Our Mission

To nourish every home in Embul Bul with fresh, fairly priced food and warm, personalised service every single day.
